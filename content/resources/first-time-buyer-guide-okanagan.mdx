Buying your first home in the Okanagan comes with a few region-specific wrinkles worth knowing about early, before you start touring homes.

## Get pre-approved before you fall in love with a listing

A mortgage pre-approval isn't the same as a pre-qualification. Pre-approval involves an actual review of your income, credit, and down payment by a lender, and gives you a real number to search against — rather than a rough guess.

## Budget for more than the purchase price

Beyond the price on the listing, plan for:

- Property Transfer Tax (often reduced or waived for qualifying first-time buyers)
- Legal or notary fees
- A home inspection
- Property insurance from day one of ownership
- Moving costs

## Understand what "subjects" protect you from

Most Okanagan offers include subject clauses — financing, inspection, and (for condos/townhomes) a review of strata documents. These aren't formalities; they're your last checkpoints before a deal becomes firm. Don't rush through them.

## Local timing matters

Inventory and competition shift by season and by community — a Kelowna condo search looks very different from a Naramata Bench acreage search. Get a local read on your specific target area rather than relying on valley-wide averages.
