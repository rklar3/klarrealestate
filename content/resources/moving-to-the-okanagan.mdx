A significant share of Okanagan buyers are relocating from somewhere else in BC or from another province entirely. A few things are worth knowing before you commit to a specific community.

## The five communities are genuinely different

It's tempting to treat "the Okanagan" as one market, but Kelowna, West Kelowna, Penticton, Vernon, and Summerland each have a distinct pace, price point, and lifestyle. See the [area guides](/areas) for a fuller comparison before narrowing your search.

## Summers are busy, winters are quieter

Expect the valley's population — and traffic, restaurant waits, and lake crowds — to swell noticeably from May through September. Winters are quieter, with Silver Star (near Vernon) and Big White (near Kelowna) drawing a ski crowd of their own.

## Commute expectations vary a lot

Kelowna has genuine city traffic at peak times, particularly around the Bennett Bridge connecting to West Kelowna. Penticton, Vernon, and Summerland are all considerably smaller and easier to get around.

## Touring virtually is normal

If you can't get out for an in-person visit before narrowing your search, virtual tours and detailed video walkthroughs are a standard part of working with buyers relocating from out of town — ask about this up front.
