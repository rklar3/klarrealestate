Pricing is the single biggest lever a seller controls, and it's also the one most often mishandled — usually by aiming too high "to leave room to negotiate."

## Why overpricing costs you more than it saves

A home that sits on the market past its first few weeks tends to accumulate a reputation, whether or not that's fair. Buyers (and their agents) track days on market, and a stale listing often ends up selling for less than a well-priced one would have, after multiple price drops signal weakness.

## What actually goes into a price recommendation

- **Recent comparable sales** — not just similar homes anywhere in the Okanagan, but truly comparable properties in your specific neighbourhood.
- **Active competition** — what else is currently on the market at a similar price point, and how your home stacks up.
- **Trend direction** — whether the local market has been moving up, down, or sideways over the past few months.
- **Condition and updates** — realistic adjustments for renovations, deferred maintenance, and anything unusual about the lot or layout.

## Staging doesn't have to mean a full redesign

Small, targeted changes — decluttering, a fresh coat of paint in a dated room, addressing an obvious deferred repair — consistently move the needle on showings and offers more than an expensive full staging package.
