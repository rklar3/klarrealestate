# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Prospective home buyers (including first-time buyers) and home sellers in the Okanagan Valley, treated as equal priorities. They browse listings, research areas and market trends, and decide whether to contact Sanam Klar or request a home valuation.

## Product Purpose
Marketing and lead-generation site for Klar Real Estate: Sanam Klar, REALTOR® with Oakwyn Realty, serving Kelowna, West Kelowna, Penticton, Vernon and Summerland. Success is a contact or valuation request.

## Positioning
Local market expertise across the Okanagan, shown through area guides, resources and a monthly market update drawn from Association of Interior REALTORS® statistics.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4; content in `data/` and `content/` (MDX), no CMS.
- Live listings from CREA DDF®; Google reviews; contact and valuation forms; mortgage calculator; FAQ.
- Market statistics come from interiorrealtors.ca, which blocks server-side fetches, so figures are updated by hand in `data/market.ts`.

## Brand Commitments
- Keep the current colour palette (terracotta, cream, sand, ink); the owner considers it right.
- REALTOR® / CREA DDF® attributions on listings must stay.

## Evidence on Hand
Real Google reviews, live DDF listings, Association of Interior REALTORS® market data. Do not invent testimonials, stats or sales figures.

## Product Principles
- Local expertise is the proof: show real area and market knowledge rather than generic claims.
- Serve buyers and sellers equally; neither path should be buried.
- Every page leads toward a clear next step: contact, valuation or listing enquiry.
- Only real data and reviews; never fabricated numbers.
